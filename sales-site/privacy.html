Privacy Policy

AtomSlice3D Studio (@atomslice3d) • Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AtomSlice3D Studio ("we", "our", or the "Application") handles information in connection with our website (https://atomslice3d.netlify.app) and our internal creator video publishing tool powered by YouTube API Services and TikTok API Services.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

AtomSlice3D Studio uses YouTube API Services exclusively as a private, single-owner creator publishing tool operated by the owner of the @atomsliced3d YouTube channel (Channel ID: UCeb2ZqPqEnXoftVhCwDPahQ). By interacting with YouTube content or features connected to our Application, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, our Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Use of TikTok API Services (Login Kit & Content Posting API)

AtomSlice3D Studio also integrates with TikTok API Services (Login Kit and Content Posting API) exclusively as a private, single-owner creator publishing tool to upload original 3D animations to the creator's own TikTok account (@atomsliced3d), either as inbox drafts (video.upload) or direct posts (video.publish). By using TikTok features connected to our Application, you agree to the TikTok Terms of Service and acknowledge the TikTok Privacy Policy.

3. Data Accessed & Purpose

  • YouTube scopes requested: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • TikTok scopes requested: user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish.
  • Purpose: Used strictly to verify creator account identity and upload 100% original 3D slicing animations rendered by AtomSlice3D Studio to our own creator channels.
  • Zero Third-Party User Data: AtomSlice3D Studio does not offer public multi-user OAuth sign-in, does not collect, access, store, aggregate, or share API data belonging to external end users, and does not sell or transfer data to any third parties.

4. Storage, Security & Retention

OAuth 2.0 credentials (access_token and refresh_token) belonging solely to the channel owner are stored encrypted in private environment secrets and are never exposed publicly or shared with third parties. No viewer personal data or platform API response payloads are stored in external databases.

5. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

In addition to our internal controls, connected account permissions granted to AtomSlice3D Studio can be revoked at any time via the Google Security Settings page or via TikTok App Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Upon revocation, all local or encrypted OAuth tokens are immediately invalidated and deleted.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

AtomSlice3D Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Contact

For any privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact the developer directly at atomslice3d@gmail.com.
