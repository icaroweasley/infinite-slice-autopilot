# Viral Playbook — English Instagram Reels Strategy

## Proven High-Retention Object Categories (Never Repeat Slugs!)

Rotate across these 4 retention archetypes to keep the feed unpredictable while maintaining the locked visual signature:

1. **Forbidden / Impossible-to-Slice Objects** (Expectation break — cutting hard/expensive items like butter):
   - Rubik's Cube, iPhone Pro, 24k Gold Bar, Billiard 8-Ball, Mechanical Watch, GameBoy / Nintendo Switch, Marble Chess Piece, Padlock, Crystal Diamond, Brick ("Is It Cake?"), Ceramic Coffee Mug, Glass Perfume Bottle.
2. **Hidden Internal Surprise Objects** (Curiosity gap until Z-slice opens):
   - Amethyst Geode (gray volcanic stone -> hollow purple crystals), Ice Block with Frozen Rose, Ice Block with Gold Coins, Matte Chocolate Block -> 7-Layer Rainbow Cake, Coconut (brown shell -> white meat -> hollow core), Dragon Fruit, Pomegranate, Matte Black Sphere -> Glowing Magma Core, Pokéball -> Mini Crystal Core.
3. **High-Contrast Layered Food** (Instant 1-second appetite appeal + vibrant micro-cubes):
   - Atlantic Salmon Fillet, Stacked Gourmet Burger, Salmon Uramaki Sushi, Dripping Honeycomb, Wagyu Marbled Steak, Layered Pancakes with Syrup & Butter, Watermelon Wedge, Avocado with Pit, Tiramisu, Neapolitan Ice Cream Block, Red Bell Pepper, Matcha Mille-Crepe Cake.
4. **Geometric / Pop-Culture Satisfying Patterns** (Hypnotic kinetic sand stripes at 1,000 cuts):
   - Crayon Pack, Layered Kinetic Sand Rainbow Prism, Striped Jawbreaker Candy, Damask Steel Ingot, Neon Circuit Block, Pixel Art Block.

## Safe Posting Cadence

- **Weeks 1–2 (New Account Warmup):** 2–3 Reels/day at fixed peak US/Global slots (`09:00`, `15:00`, `21:00 UTC`). Avoid posting 24 Reels/day on a new account (triggers Instagram spam throttling).
- **Weeks 3+ (Scaled):** 4–6 Reels/day across rotating environments (`classic-viral`, `luxury-obsidian`, `glass-lab`, `tropical-leaf`, `pro-kitchen`, `frozen-block`).
